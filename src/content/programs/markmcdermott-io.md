The personal counterpart to this studio. Longer-form writing about software, AI,
attention and neurodiversity — the thinking that ends up shaping what gets built
here.

## Why it's separate

A studio site and a personal site want different things. This one is a shop
window: what the studio makes, and how to reach it. That one is a notebook, and a
notebook reads better when it isn't also trying to sell you something.

## How it's built

Swiss-typographic Astro and Tailwind, content in Markdown, deployed static. No
database, no CMS — posts are files, which means the whole archive is greppable and
survives any platform I might otherwise have rented.
