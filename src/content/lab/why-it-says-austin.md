I'm an East-coaster, born and raised, who's also lived in Austin, Texas for two decades. I've lived in Austin now as long as I lived in Pennsylvania. Austin isn't exactly known for it's coffee or anything, but really coffee, web development and Austin are an amazing combo.

I wish I knew how many hours I've spent in Austin coffeeshops working on my laptop.  Wouldn't be surprised if it's been year of my life or something ridiculous. I worked a bunch of random non-coffeeshop type jobs--mowed lawns, worked at an animal shelter and was a grocery store (Central Market) cashier. Around then I decided I probably needed a career rather than just jobs. I had never used my econ degree from Penn State and didn't really have any interest in pursuing econ-related career paths.

I had a cousin who was a project manager in web development and was making a lot of money and recommended I get in the industry. I wasn't sure where to start. I started learning PHP on my own. I built a artist portfolio website for an co-worker who was an artist. I also built a memorial site for a co-worker who'd passed away (maybe suicide? It was a strange situation and I never did find out).
